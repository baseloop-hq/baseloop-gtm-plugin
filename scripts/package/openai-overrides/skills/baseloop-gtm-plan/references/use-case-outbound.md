# Outbound

This package does not include the outbound campaign recipe.

When a goal routes here (a campaign to a slice of the market, or people to add to a sequence):

- Plan the parts the other recipes cover: the account list and its tiers with [use-case-tam-sourcing.md](./use-case-tam-sourcing.md), the people and their contact data with [use-case-prospecting.md](./use-case-prospecting.md), and the event that makes someone relevant with [use-case-signals.md](./use-case-signals.md).
- End the plan at a reviewed list in the table and in the CRM. Do not plan campaign enrolment or message copy from this file.
- Tell the user in one line that campaign planning is not part of this package, and that the list is ready for the tool they send from.
